FAQ

Straight answers.

The questions contractors ask us most before signing.

What is included in your rate?

Visa, labour card, medical insurance, WPS salary, accommodation, daily transport and PPE. Your quote lists each item, so there are no extra fees later.

Are you a licensed manpower supplier?

Yes. We are licensed with MOHRE and send our licence details with every quote, so you can check them before you sign.

How fast can workers reach my site?

For most trades, 24 to 72 hours. Large crews or specialist roles can take longer, and we tell you the real date upfront.

What if a worker is not the right fit?

Tell your ARC supervisor. We replace the worker and keep your crew at full strength.

How do you make sure workers are paid?

Every worker is paid monthly through the UAE Wage Protection System (WPS). Unpaid wages never become your site's problem.

What contract lengths do you offer?

Daily, monthly, long-term or per project. You can scale the crew up or down as the work changes.

Which Emirates do you cover?

All seven: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah.
