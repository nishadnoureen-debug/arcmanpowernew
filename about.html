Who we are

Good projects start with good people.

ARC Manpower is a UAE manpower supply company. We provide skilled, semi-skilled and general workers to construction, MEP, oil and gas, facility, hospitality and logistics clients.

We handle the whole lifecycle of every worker: sourcing, trade testing, visas, medical insurance, payroll, housing and daily transport. Our clients deal with one supplier and one invoice.

We follow UAE labour law in full and pay every worker on time. That is why clients come back to us project after project.
